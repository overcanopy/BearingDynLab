# Validation Cases

Validation should be developed from simple checks to literature-level benchmarks.

## Level 1: Mathematical consistency

- Zero indentation gives zero contact force.
- Negative indentation gives zero contact force.
- Contact force increases monotonically with indentation.
- Symmetric roller arrangement gives symmetric force response.

## Level 2: Static bearing load distribution

- Compare load-zone pattern with classical rolling-bearing theory.
- Check sensitivity to radial clearance.
- Check sensitivity to number of rolling elements.

## Level 3: Dynamic force signatures

- Prescribed rotating load zone.
- Outer-race defect excitation.
- Inner-race defect excitation.
- Roller defect excitation.

## Level 4: Rotor-bearing benchmark

- Jeffcott rotor supported by nonlinear bearing force.
- Compare linearized and nonlinear support predictions.
- Check unbalance response and resonance behavior.

## Level 5: Experimental comparison

Possible experimental validation data:

- bearing vibration signal under known shaft speed,
- defected bearing dataset,
- measured acceleration spectrum,
- measured load and speed conditions.

The project should record all validation assumptions, units, and parameter sources explicitly.
